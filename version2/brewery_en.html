CHOWOL BREWERY TASTING

Traditional Korean makgeolli
at a brewery in the heart of Seoul

Chowol is a small Seoul brewery making makgeolli from just Haenam rice, nuruk, water — and time.

Book a tasting → Getting here 한국어
01 — Brewery

Tasting session

In this tasting session we introduce Korea’s traditional fermentation culture and how makgeolli is made, then you taste and compare four makgeolli, each with its own character. With a few simple pairing snacks, it’s a time to discover the many flavours and aromas of makgeolli.

  • Tasting of 4 makgeolli
  • Pairing snacks
  • Intro to traditional fermentation & brewing

05 — FAQ

Booking & cancellation

Can I join without booking?

We recommend booking in advance. Same-day spots may be available depending on bookings.

What is the cancellation policy?

Full refund if cancelled at least 24 hours before the session starts. Cancellations within 24 hours are non-refundable. All times are in Korea Standard Time (KST).

Can I change my booking?

Please contact us in advance to change your booking.

Can minors come along?

The tasting is for ages 19 and over (Korean drinking age). If you plan to visit with someone under 19, please contact us first.

Is the session in English?

Sessions are held in Korean or English.
